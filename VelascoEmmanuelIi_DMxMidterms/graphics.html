Graphics

What are Graphics?

Graphics are visual images or designs that can be displayed on a screen or be printed. They are used to communicate ideas or enhance various forms of media. These play a role in UIs, digital art, web designs, and many more.

There are many types of graphics. For 2D Graphics, Raster and Vector are its main types.

Raster Graphics

What are Raster Graphics?

Raster graphics are also known as "bitmap graphics." As the name suggests, they use a grid of pixels, each containing color information, to form an image. The higher the resolution of the image, the more pixels there are.

Raster graphics can display a wide range of colors, making them ideal for digital photographs. This also causes them to have large file sizes, but they make up for it with easier sharing and display. Unfortunately, resizing a raster graphic will lose image quality.

Common file types include .jpg and .png.

Vector Graphics

What are Vector Graphics?

Unlike raster graphics, vector graphics use mathematical equations to define the lines, shapes, and colors of an image.

Vector graphics are predominantly used for digital illustrations and logo designs due to their capability of maintaining their resolution regardless of image size. However, they cannot render complex image details such as textures and shading that digital photographs have. As such, vector graphics tend to have small file sizes.

Common file types include .svg and .eps.
